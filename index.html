Bio

Hi, I am Yun Lin, also known as Sharkyun. I am a Data Scientist at Foxconn Interconnect Technology (FIT), working across research and AI application for industrial computer vision, smart manufacturing inspection, and practical data products.

My work spans label-efficient domain adaptation research, computer vision model development, Azure DevOps CI/CD pipelines for model deployment, and agentic assistants across business units. Before FIT, I gained data experience through roles at Neurobit, Applify AI, Cupoy.com, UCLA Recreation, CodingApe, and College of San Mateo.
